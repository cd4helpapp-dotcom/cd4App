## Upload a report or prescription
Open the Reports tab and tap Add. Choose Test report or Prescription, then take a photo, pick from your gallery, or choose a PDF. For the best result use good light and keep the whole page in the frame. The file can be up to 8 MB, as PDF, JPG or PNG.

## How long report reading takes
A photo usually takes about 15 seconds and a long PDF can take up to a minute. You can leave the screen while it runs. The result appears on the report.

## What you get from a report
CD4 AI explains the report in simple words, lists your test values with their normal ranges, and marks the values that are high or low. For a prescription it lists each medicine with its dose and timing. You can then ask questions about that report. AI can make mistakes reading a document, so check values against your original and talk to your doctor before changing any medicine.

## Report upload limits
The free plan allows 5 report uploads every 24 hours. Pro allows more. The upload screen shows how many you have left.

## Who can see my reports
Only you can see the reports you upload. A doctor sees the summary linked to an appointment you booked with them. You can delete a report at any time.

## What CD4 AI can do
CD4 AI can answer health questions about symptoms, medicines, reports, diet and mental wellbeing, take your history before a consultation, and find verified doctors and open slots. It works in English and Hindi, in text and by voice, and it replies in the language you are using.

## What CD4 AI cannot do
CD4 AI is an AI, not a doctor. It does not diagnose or prescribe. In an emergency, call 112 or go to the nearest hospital.

## Message limits and Pro
Free and Pro plans have daily limits for AI messages and voice sessions, and Pro's limits are higher. Simple greetings do not count towards your limit. The Upgrade to Pro screen shows current plans and prices.

## Change the app language or theme
Settings, then App Language, switches between English and Hindi. Settings, then Appearance, chooses Light, Dark or System.

## Delete my account
Settings, then Help and Support, then Delete account. Your account and health data are permanently deleted within 30 days. You can also write to support@cd4.app.

## Contact support
Settings, then Help and Support, or write to support@cd4.app. Include your registered email or phone number, and the payment ID for a payment question.
