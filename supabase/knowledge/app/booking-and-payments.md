## How to book a doctor
Open the Appointments tab and choose Find a doctor. Filter by city or department, open a doctor and pick an open slot. The next screen shows the fee. Your booking is confirmed once the payment succeeds. You can also ask CD4 AI to find a doctor, and it takes you to the same payment screen when you choose a slot. CD4 AI cannot confirm a booking without payment.

## Consultation fee
Each doctor sets their own fee. You see the exact amount before you pay, and you are charged only that amount. Payments go through Razorpay. CD4 does not store your card or bank details.

## Booking the same doctor again
You can book the same doctor once in 30 days. If you already booked that doctor in the last 30 days, the app tells you the date you can book again.

## What happens after booking
Your booking appears under My appointments. The doctor gets a short summary of your concern, prepared from your conversation with CD4 AI, so the consultation starts faster. Your consultation happens in the app through chat and call with your doctor on the day of the appointment.

## Payment taken but no booking showing
Do not pay again. Check My appointments after a minute, because the booking finishes on its own after a successful payment. If someone else booked the slot just before your payment finished, the payment is refunded automatically. If it still looks wrong, contact support with the payment ID from Payment history.

## Cancel a consultation
Open My appointments and tap Cancel on the booking. You can cancel up to 2 hours before the slot. Your fee is refunded in full to your original payment method, which usually takes 5 to 7 working days. Within 2 hours of the slot the app does not allow changes, so please contact support.

## Reschedule a consultation
Open My appointments and tap Reschedule, then choose another open slot with the same doctor. You can do this up to 2 hours before the slot. There is nothing more to pay, and your doctor is told about the new time.

## If the doctor cancels
If your doctor cancels, you get a notification and a full refund. You can then book another slot or another doctor.

## Payment history
Settings, then Payment history, lists your consultation and subscription payments with their status: Paid, Refunded or Failed, and a payment ID you can share with support.
