## What the flu is
Seasonal flu is a respiratory infection caused by influenza viruses. It spreads through droplets from coughs and sneezes and through contaminated hands. Most people recover without treatment.

## Flu symptoms
Sudden fever, usually dry cough, headache, muscle and joint pain, feeling very unwell, sore throat and runny nose. Symptoms begin 1 to 4 days after infection and usually last about a week. The cough can last 2 weeks or more.

## Who is at higher risk from flu
Pregnant women, children under 5, older people, and people with long-term conditions or a weak immune system.

## Flu treatment
With mild illness, stay home, rest, drink fluids and treat fever. People at high risk or with severe symptoms should get medical care quickly, as they may need antiviral medicine. See a doctor if mild symptoms get worse.

## Preventing the flu
Yearly vaccination is the best prevention. Also wash hands often, cover coughs and sneezes, stay home when unwell, and avoid close contact with sick people.
