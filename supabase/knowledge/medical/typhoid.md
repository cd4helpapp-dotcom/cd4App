## What typhoid is
Typhoid is a life-threatening infection caused by Salmonella Typhi bacteria. It usually spreads through contaminated food or water. Children are at highest risk, especially where water and sanitation are poor.

## Typhoid symptoms
Prolonged high fever, tiredness, headache, nausea, abdominal pain, and constipation or diarrhoea. Some people get a rash. It can be confirmed with a blood test. Severe cases can lead to serious complications or death.

## Typhoid treatment
Typhoid is treated with antibiotics prescribed by a doctor, and resistance is common. Take the full course even after you feel better, because a person can still carry the bacteria. Wash hands with soap after using the toilet, avoid preparing food for others, and have a doctor confirm the bacteria have gone.

## Preventing typhoid
Safe drinking water, good sanitation, hygiene among food handlers and typhoid vaccination all help. Eat food cooked thoroughly and served hot. Drink boiled or pasteurized milk. Boil doubtful water, avoid ice unless made from safe water, and wash and peel fruit and vegetables.
