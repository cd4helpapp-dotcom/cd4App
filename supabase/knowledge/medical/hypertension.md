## What high blood pressure is
Hypertension is blood pressure of 140/90 mmHg or higher, confirmed on two different days. Most people have no symptoms, so the only way to know is to have it measured.

## High blood pressure warning signs
Very high blood pressure is usually 180/120 or higher. Seek care immediately for severe headache, chest pain, dizziness, difficulty breathing, nausea, vomiting, vision changes, confusion, nosebleeds or an abnormal heart rhythm.

## High blood pressure risk factors
Too much salt, saturated and trans fats, too few fruit and vegetables, inactivity, tobacco, alcohol, being overweight and air pollution. Family history, age over 65, diabetes and kidney disease also raise the risk.

## High blood pressure complications
It can damage the heart, causing chest pain, heart attack, heart failure or irregular heartbeat. It can also cause stroke and kidney failure.

## Lowering blood pressure
Limit salt to under 2 grams a day, eat more vegetables and fruit, do 150 minutes of moderate activity a week, lose weight if overweight, avoid tobacco and limit alcohol. Some people need medicine. Take it as prescribed, do not miss doses, and do not share it.
