## What dengue is
Dengue is a viral infection spread by the bite of infected mosquitoes, mainly Aedes aegypti. It is most common in tropical and subtropical areas, mostly in cities and towns.

## Dengue symptoms
Most people have mild or no symptoms and recover in 1 to 2 weeks. Symptoms usually begin 4 to 10 days after infection and last 2 to 7 days. Common symptoms are high fever, severe headache, pain behind the eyes, muscle and joint pain, nausea, vomiting, swollen glands and rash. Tiredness can last several weeks after recovery. A second dengue infection raises the risk of severe dengue.

## Dengue warning signs
See a doctor immediately for severe abdominal pain, persistent vomiting, rapid breathing, bleeding from the gums or nose, blood in vomit or stool, extreme thirst, pale and cold skin, restlessness or weakness. These signs often appear after the fever has gone.

## Dengue treatment
There is no specific treatment. Rest and drink plenty of fluids. Paracetamol can manage pain. Avoid ibuprofen and aspirin, because they can increase the risk of bleeding. Severe dengue often needs hospital care, and early care greatly lowers the risk of death.

## Preventing dengue
Avoid mosquito bites, especially during the day, with covering clothes, nets, window screens and repellents containing DEET, Picaridin or IR3535. Stop mosquitoes breeding: remove containers that hold water, and clean and cover stored water every week.
