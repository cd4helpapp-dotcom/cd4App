## What diabetes is
Diabetes is a long-term disease in which the pancreas makes too little insulin or the body cannot use insulin well. Type 1 needs insulin every day. Type 2 is the most common, in more than 95% of people with diabetes. Gestational diabetes is raised blood sugar during pregnancy.

## Diabetes symptoms
Feeling very thirsty, urinating more often, blurred vision, tiredness and unintended weight loss. Type 2 symptoms can be mild and go unnoticed for years.

## Diabetes risk factors
Type 2 is linked to being overweight, too little exercise and genetics. Women who have gestational diabetes have a higher risk of pregnancy complications and of type 2 diabetes later.

## Diabetes complications
Blindness, kidney failure, heart attack, stroke and lower limb amputation. Regular foot care, kidney screening and eye checks help find problems early.

## Preventing diabetes
Lifestyle changes are the most effective way to prevent or delay type 2 diabetes: keep a healthy weight, do at least 150 minutes of moderate exercise a week, eat a healthy diet low in sugar and saturated fat, and avoid tobacco.

## Diabetes testing and treatment
Diabetes is diagnosed with a blood glucose test. A healthy lifestyle is a key part of treatment. Some people with type 2 also need medicines a doctor prescribes, such as metformin or insulin.
