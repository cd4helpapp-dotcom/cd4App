## What depression is
Depression is a common mental health condition, different from ordinary mood changes. It can happen to anyone and can affect relationships, study and work.

## Depression symptoms
Feeling sad, irritable or empty, or losing interest or pleasure, for most of the day, nearly every day, for at least two weeks. Also poor concentration, guilt or low self-worth, hopelessness, thoughts of dying or suicide, disturbed sleep, changes in appetite or weight, and low energy.

## Depression treatment
Effective treatments exist. Psychological treatments such as cognitive behavioural therapy come first and can be combined with medicines for moderate and severe depression. Antidepressants are not needed for mild depression, and need a doctor's guidance.

## Helping yourself with depression
Keep doing activities you enjoy, stay connected with family and friends, exercise, even a short walk, keep regular eating and sleeping habits, avoid or reduce alcohol, and talk to someone you trust.

## Thoughts of suicide
If you have thoughts of suicide, talk to someone you trust or a doctor or counsellor. If you are in immediate danger of harming yourself, contact emergency services (112) or a crisis line right away.
