## What asthma is
Asthma is a long-term lung condition in which inflamed, tight airways make breathing harder. It affects all ages. It cannot be cured, but it can be controlled.

## Asthma symptoms
Persistent cough, often at night, wheezing, breathlessness and a tight chest. Symptoms come and go and often worsen at night or with exercise. A sudden, significant worsening is an asthma attack.

## Asthma triggers
Colds, weather changes, dust, smoke, fumes, pollen, animal fur and feathers, and strong soaps or perfumes.

## Asthma treatment
Asthma is usually controlled with inhalers: bronchodilators such as salbutamol open the airways, and steroids such as beclometasone reduce inflammation. A spacer makes aerosol inhalers easier to use. Your doctor may give an asthma action plan, including how to step up treatment when symptoms worsen. Severe attacks may need emergency care.
