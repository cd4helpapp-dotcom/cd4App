## Heart attack symptoms
Pain or discomfort in the centre of the chest, and/or in the arms, left shoulder, elbows, jaw or back. Also difficulty breathing, nausea or vomiting, light-headedness or faintness, cold sweat and paleness. Women more often have shortness of breath, nausea, vomiting, and back or jaw pain. Anyone with these symptoms should get medical care immediately.

## Stroke symptoms
Sudden weakness or numbness of the face, arm or leg, most often on one side. Also sudden confusion, trouble speaking or understanding, trouble seeing, trouble walking, dizziness, loss of balance, severe headache with no known cause, fainting or unconsciousness. Anyone with these symptoms should get medical care immediately.

## Heart disease risk factors
Unhealthy diet, inactivity, tobacco, harmful alcohol use and air pollution, which can show up as raised blood pressure, blood sugar and blood fats, and being overweight.

## Preventing heart disease
Most heart disease can be prevented: stop tobacco, reduce salt, eat more fruit and vegetables, be physically active and avoid harmful alcohol use. Treating high blood pressure, diabetes and high blood fats also lowers the risk.
