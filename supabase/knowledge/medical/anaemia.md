## What anaemia is
Anaemia means the blood has fewer red blood cells or less haemoglobin than normal, so it carries less oxygen. It is a sign of an underlying cause, and is diagnosed by a blood test.

## Anaemia causes
Iron deficiency from diet is the most common nutritional cause. Others are low vitamin A, folate or vitamin B12, blood loss from heavy periods or childbirth, infections such as malaria and TB, chronic diseases, and inherited conditions such as thalassaemia and sickle cell disorders.

## Anaemia symptoms
Tiredness, dizziness, cold hands and feet, headache and shortness of breath, especially on effort. Severe anaemia can cause pale skin and nails, rapid breathing and heartbeat, and dizziness on standing.

## Who is at higher risk of anaemia
Children under 5, menstruating girls and women, and pregnant women and women who have recently given birth.

## Anaemia treatment
Treatment depends on the cause. Eat a varied diet with iron-rich foods. Take supplements only if a qualified health provider recommends them.
