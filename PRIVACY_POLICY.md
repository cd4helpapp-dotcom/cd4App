# Privacy Policy for CD4

**Last Updated:** August 23, 2026  
**Effective Date:** August 23, 2026

Welcome to **CD4** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal and health-related information. This Privacy Policy explains how CD4 collects, uses, processes, and safeguards your information when you use the CD4 mobile application and associated services (the "Service").

By accessing or using CD4, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide you with telemedicine, doctor consultations, AI-assisted health triage, and appointment booking services:

#### a. Personal Information
* **Account Details:** Name, email address, phone number, age, gender, and profile photo when you sign up or log in.
* **Authentication Data:** Google Sign-In authentication tokens and session identifiers.

#### b. Health & Medical Data
* **User-Provided Symptoms & Queries:** Information you enter or speak regarding your health concerns, symptoms, and medical history.
* **Medical Records & Prescriptions:** PDF/image reports, lab results, prescriptions, and consultation notes that you upload or receive from doctors.

#### c. Audio, Voice & Media Data
* **Microphone & Voice Input:** Voice recordings and speech streams captured when you interact with the AI Voice Assistant, voice triage, or during audio/video calls with doctors.
* **Camera & Photos:** Video feed during teleconsultation calls and images captured when uploading medical documents.

#### d. Location Information
* **Precise & Approximate Location:** Coarse/fine location data used solely to find, filter, and recommend nearby verified doctors, clinics, and hospitals.

#### e. Transaction & Payment Information
* **Payment Details:** Transaction IDs and payment status processed securely via authorized third-party payment gateways (Razorpay). We do not store your credit/debit card numbers or bank credentials on our servers.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:
1. **Facilitating Teleconsultations:** Enabling real-time audio/video consultations and chat between patients and verified healthcare professionals.
2. **AI Health Guidance & Triage:** Providing preliminary, informational health guidance and voice-based symptom analysis.
3. **Appointment Booking & Management:** Scheduling appointments and optionally syncing reminders with your device calendar.
4. **Prescription & Record Management:** Storing and organizing your medical records for easy access and doctor review.
5. **Notifications & Alerts:** Sending appointment reminders, incoming doctor call notifications, and critical health updates.
6. **Security & Compliance:** Preventing fraud, ensuring data integrity, and complying with applicable healthcare regulations.

---

### 3. Permissions We Request and Why

CD4 requests the following device permissions with transparent justifications:
* **Microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`):** Required for speaking with the AI Voice Assistant and voice/video teleconsultations with doctors.
* **Camera (`CAMERA`):** Required for live video consultations with doctors and scanning/uploading medical records.
* **Location (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` / `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`):** Used to display nearby doctors, clinics, and relevant regional health services.
* **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Used to deliver incoming call alerts and appointment reminders.
* **Calendar (`READ_CALENDAR` / `WRITE_CALENDAR`):** Used to save your confirmed doctor consultations directly to your device calendar.
* **Storage / Photos (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / Document Picker):** Used to select and upload lab reports, prescriptions, or profile photos.

---

### 4. Third-Party Service Providers

We share data with trusted third-party providers strictly to operate our services:
* **Database & Cloud Infrastructure:** Supabase (encrypted cloud storage and authentication).
* **Teleconsultation & Real-Time Communication:** Agora / WebRTC (encrypted video and audio call transmission).
* **AI & Natural Language Processing:** OpenAI / Realtime API (processing queries and generating speech responses).
* **Payment Processing:** Razorpay (PCI-DSS compliant payment processing).
* **Authentication & Notifications:** Google Sign-In and Expo (secure authentication and push notifications).

We **do NOT sell, rent, or trade** your personal or health data to advertisers or third-party brokers.

---

### 5. Data Security & Storage

* All data transmitted between your device and our servers is protected using industry-standard **TLS/SSL (HTTPS/WSS) encryption**.
* Health records and sensitive personal data are stored in secure databases with strict Role-Based Access Controls (RBAC).
* Audio from AI voice sessions and speech recognition is processed in real time and is not used for behavioral advertising.

---

### 6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

* **Data Retention:** We retain your account and health data for as long as your account is active or as necessary to fulfill healthcare documentation and legal obligations.
* **Account & Data Deletion:** You have the right to permanently delete your account and all associated health data at any time.
  * **In-App:** Go to **Settings → Help & Support → Delete Account** and confirm deletion.
  * **Support Request:** Email us at **support@cd4.app** with the subject *"Account Deletion Request"*. Your account, chat history, uploaded medical documents, and personal details will be permanently purged within 30 days.

---

### 7. Medical & Emergency Disclaimer

> **IMPORTANT:** CD4 is a healthcare technology platform connecting users with licensed doctors and providing AI-assisted health information.
> * The AI Voice Assistant and report summaries are for **informational and triage support only** and do **NOT** constitute medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
> * If you are experiencing a medical emergency (e.g., severe chest pain, shortness of breath, loss of consciousness), immediately contact your local emergency services (e.g., **112 / 102 / 108**) or visit the nearest hospital emergency room.
